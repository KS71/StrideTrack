StrideTrack is a simple app for tracking your daily steps and walking goals.

The app works fully offline with no account required. If you don't want to create an account, you can instead make a local backup directly on your phone. If you want to sync your data across devices, you can optionally create a profile, which connects to Supabase for account storage. Connecting a Google Health Connect account is also optional and only used if you choose to enable it.

Features:
- Track walking goals
- Works without an account
- Local backup on your device
- Optional cloud sync via Supabase
- Optional Google Health Connect integration
